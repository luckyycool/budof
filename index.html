Paul Graham’s "Default Alive" Framework Explained

In his famous 2015 essay, Y Combinator founder Paul Graham asked startup founders a simple question: "Are you default alive or default dead?"


The Equation: If your monthly revenue continues growing at its current percentage, will revenue surpass your monthly gross burn before your bank balance hits zero? If YES, you are Default Alive — you can survive without external venture funding. If NO, you are Default Dead — unless you raise more capital or cut expenses, your company will perish on a fixed date.


Budof runs this exact calculation dynamically across a 36-month horizon, showing you the exact month your startup achieves self-sustainability or when you must make strategic headcount adjustments.
